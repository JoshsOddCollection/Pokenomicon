# Errors

---

## **Quick Summary**
- Repeating errors generally require proof of **three copies**.
- Extremely minor, microscopic, or reproducible errors are **not accepted**.

---

## What makes an error a repeating error?
An error is classified as repeating if there is evidence it is not a one-of-a-kind occurrence.

**Standard requirement:**<br>
When submitting a new repeating error, you must provide proof that at least three copies of the error exist. This requirement helps ensure the error is genuinely repeating and not the result of post-production damage, alteration, or a one-off obstruction.

**Exceptions:**<br>
Because cards are printed on sheets containing multiple copies of each card, some errors are known to repeat by default. For these error types, proof of a single copy is sufficient:
- Holo shifts (misaligned opaque layer)
- Inverted backs

Another weird edge case is **End of Holo Roll** cards. These cards must be verified on a case-by-case basis. Since this error only affects cards on the edge of the sheet, verification is entirely dependent upon the sheet that the card comes from (for example, Fossil sheets have three Kabutops on the very left side of the sheet, so only a single copy is required as proof.). Different severities (how far into the card the holo roll is missing) can also be used to prove that multiple copies exist since each sheet will have a different severity.

**Design errors:**<br>
Although design errors are inherently printed multiple times, multiple verified copies are still required. This ensures the error is truly a design error and not another error type (for example, an obstruction covering text or a hickey causing letters to appear incorrect).

---

## Repeating errors that will be denied
Some errors may technically be repeating but may be excluded due to being extremely minor. All errors will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, with these criteria serving as the baseline for acceptance. An error variant may be denied even if it does not match the criteria below; we will update the criteria if an error is denied for a reason not currently listed.

Here is a list of errors that will not be accepted:
- **Microscopic errors** - if it can't be seen by the naked eye then it is most likely too small.
- **Holo bleeds** - these usually apply to entire sets instead of individual cards and are not very interesting or unique. Exceptions will be made for cards that are extreme or for cards that are actually missing the opaque layer.
- **Miscuts / Off-Center** - miscuts usually apply to an entire set with varying severities and almost every single card can be found in a miscut/off-center variant due to poor quality control. For that reason, these cards will not be included as repeat errors.
- **Reproducible "errors"**
    - **Not Factory Cut (NFC) cards** - these cards are not legitimate errors and do not belong in the database.
    - **Missing gold foil cards** - there are ways to strip the gold foil from cards so there is no way to verify the authenticity of these cards. Since these cards cannot be verified, they will be denied.
    - **Sunfaded cards** - these are not actually errors and are post-factory UV damage. These can often be confused with low-ink or missing-ink cards.

## Discovering new repeating errors
If you are unfamiliar with identifying repeating errors, the following guidelines explain which types of errors tend to repeat, and how often.

**Always repeats:**
- **Design errors** - since multiple cards are printed on each sheet, design errors are guaranteed to be repeating.
- **Holo shifts** - since multiple cards are printed on each sheet, if the opaque layer is misaligned on one card, then every other card on the sheet will have the same amount of misalignment.
- **Inverted backs** - since the entire sheet is printed with the same cardstock, and multiple of the same card are printed on each sheet, inverted back errors are guaranteed to be repeating.

**Often repeats:**
- **Printer hickeys** - due to dust particles and ink sticking to the rollers, printer hickeys are usually going to repeat, but it may be difficult to find multiple copies depending on how large the printer hickey is.

**Rarely repeats:**
- **Liquid obstructions** - sometimes liquid obstructions repeat in the same location, but usually appear across different sections of multiple cards due to a spill of some kind. If a liquid obstruction is known to repeat on a card, even if it's in a different location on the card, it will be accepted as a repeating error.

**Almost never repeats:**
- **Retained obstructions** - since retained obstructions are physically present on the card, it is _very_ rare that a retained obstruction is repeated. These are usually one-of-a-kind errors that present in unique ways.
